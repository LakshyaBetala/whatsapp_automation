# ASVA - Data Processing Agreement (one page)

Plain-language agreement between the shop (you) and ASVA. Under India's DPDP Act,
the shop is the Data Fiduciary (you own your customers' data) and ASVA is the Data
Processor (we handle it for you, only to do the job you hired us for). Copy rules:
plain language, no em/en dashes, English + Hinglish.

---

## ENGLISH

**What data we handle for you:** your customers' names, WhatsApp numbers, and their
outstanding amounts - read from your Tally, only so ASVA can send reminders and
show you who to chase.

**What we do with it:** send payment reminders on your own WhatsApp number, capture
replies/screenshots into your Payments tab, and show your dashboard. Nothing else.

**What we never do:** we never sell or share your data with anyone; we never post to
your Tally on our own (you confirm every entry); we never touch money.

**Where it lives:** on your Tally (your PC) and on ASVA's secured server. We keep it
only while you use ASVA.

**Your rights (and your customers'):**
- Ask us to delete all your data at any time - we erase it fully (delete-on-request).
- Any customer can reply STOP to stop reminders (consent withdrawal), instantly.
- Old message logs and generated PDFs are purged automatically.

**If there is ever a data breach:** we tell you promptly (within 72 hours) and act to
contain it.

**Contact for data questions or deletion:** <your support email/number>.

By using ASVA on the free pilot, you agree to the above. Either side can end it any
time; on end, we delete your data on request.

---

## HINGLISH

**Hum aapka kaunsa data handle karte hain:** aapke customers ke naam, WhatsApp number,
aur unka baaki - aapki Tally se padha jaata hai, sirf isliye taaki ASVA reminder bhej
sake aur aapko bataye kis kis ko chase karna hai.

**Uska kya karte hain:** aapke apne WhatsApp number se payment reminder bhejte hain,
customer ke reply/screenshot ko Payments tab mein laate hain, aur dashboard dikhate
hain. Iske alawa kuch nahi.

**Kya kabhi nahi karte:** aapka data kisi ko na bechte na share karte hain; aapki Tally
mein khud kabhi post nahi karte (har entry aap confirm karte hain); paise ko haath nahi
lagate.

**Kahan rehta hai:** aapki Tally (aapka PC) aur ASVA ke secure server par. Jab tak aap
ASVA use karte hain, tab tak hi rakha jaata hai.

**Aapke (aur aapke customers ke) adhikaar:**
- Kabhi bhi apna saara data delete karwa sakte hain - hum poora mita dete hain.
- Koi bhi customer STOP reply karke reminder turant band karwa sakta hai.
- Purane message log aur PDF apne aap delete ho jaate hain.

**Data leak hone par:** hum aapko jaldi (72 ghante ke andar) batate hain aur rokne ke
liye kaam karte hain.

**Data ya deletion ke sawaal ke liye:** <aapka support email/number>.

Free pilot par ASVA use karke aap upar likhe se sehmat hain. Dono taraf kabhi bhi khatam
kar sakte hain; khatam hone par, request par hum aapka data delete kar dete hain.

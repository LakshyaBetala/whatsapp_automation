The problem

Distributors sell on credit, then spend their life chasing it.

A wholesaler sells on 30–160 day terms. Tens of lakhs sit locked in a long list of debtors. Chasing is manual, awkward and inconsistent — so DSO creeps up, working capital stays trapped, and you pay interest on money your customers owe you.

Today's tools don't fix it. Tally only shows the outstanding. Add-on apps text from a generic number nobody trusts and bill per message. Nobody closes the loop from ledger to money in the bank.

How it works

Three parts. Your WhatsApp never leaves your PC.

Each part does only its job. The shop's WhatsApp stays on the shop's own connection, so the number is never at risk.

ON THE SHOP'S PC
The reader

A small app sits next to Tally, reads the ledger, and delivers the server's queued messages from your own WhatsApp. Holds no keys.

IN THE CLOUD

The command surface

Run the whole thing by texting.

No dashboard to log into. The owner texts the assistant; the customer self-serves on the same chat.

Owner commands

Customer commands
